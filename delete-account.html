Delete Account & Data

Last updated: 10 August 2026

TaterMind ("the App") is developed by TATERMIND, a sole proprietorship registered in Singapore. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  • Open the App and go to Profile to find your Player ID.
  • Email support@tatermind.com with the subject "Delete my account" and include your Player ID.
  • We'll confirm your identity via the Player ID and process the deletion. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done, typically within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes:

  • Your anonymous account and Player ID
  • Gameplay data — scores, moves, completion times, game mode history
  • Experience points, level, badges, and streak history
  • Your display name and player-vs-player match history
  • Your linked Google or Apple account email (if you'd backed up your progress)

What we may retain

We may retain limited records where required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution (for example, purchase/entitlement records related to the "Remove ads" pack, which are also independently held by the app store you purchased through). These are not used for any other purpose and are not linked back to your gameplay history after deletion.

Questions

Contact us at support@tatermind.com for anything else related to your data.
